Shape Shifters

Place your shapes. Protect your territory. Outplay your opponent.

Select a shape, then tap a grid cell to place it. Each player has one triangle.

How to play

Two players take turns placing shapes on the grid. Triangles eliminate other shapes touched by their three tips, including opposing triangles, but squares are immune to triangle tips. Squares act as barriers. A circle fully enclosed by a square triggers an ultimate victory; if both players achieve this in the same resolution, they share the victory. This first prototype uses local pass-and-play and simplified one-cell shape placement.
