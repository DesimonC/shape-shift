        The shape of the game

        How to play

        Each player secretly places one triangle, square, circle and line barrier on the same 10×10 arena. Tap a grid intersection to place or move a selected shape. Rotate in 15° steps. Your own shapes may touch, but cannot overlap; no more than 10% of any shape may leave the board.

        △ Triangle — the attacker. Only its three tips destroy vulnerable opponent shapes they touch. Its edges and centre do not attack. Triangles can destroy each other.

        □ Square — the survivor. Always immune to triangle tips. A square does not replace the line barrier.

        ━ Line — the barrier. Protects any shape it touches, including an opponent's. It sacrifices itself to absorb triangle attacks on those shapes, or tips that touch the barrier itself. Protection applies throughout the simultaneous reveal.

        ○ Circle — the bonus. Small enough to fit inside a square. Earns double clear-area points if it survives.

        One big reveal

        Lock in all four shapes. In pass-and-play, hand the device over before the next player begins. Opponent placements stay hidden until everyone is ready. The grid fades away and all triangle attacks happen simultaneously, so an eliminated triangle still attacks in that reveal.

        Ultimate Victory

        A surviving circle completely inside another player's square wins immediately, overriding scores. If several players achieve this, they share Ultimate Victory.

        Scoring

        Otherwise, each surviving shape earns up to 100 points: the percentage of its full area that is on the board and clear of surviving opponent shapes. Circles earn double (up to 200). Destroyed shapes and sacrificed barriers earn zero. Highest total wins, up to 500 points. Equal totals share victory; scores are rounded for display only.

        One placement and reveal is one game. Play again for a fresh arena. Online rooms support 2–4 people, expire after two hours and can be rejoined from the same browser. Computer games work without an online room.

        Leave this game?

        Your unfinished local placements will be lost. Online players can return using this browser.
